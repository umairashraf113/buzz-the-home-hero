
## Step 3: Connect Domain to Site 🔗
Once you have bought the domain (e.g., from Namecheap) and deployed the site (Step 2):

1.  **Log in to Netlify** (where you dragged the folder).
2.  Go to **Domain Settings** > **Add Custom Domain**.
3.  Type your new domain (e.g., `www.bzbuilt.ca`).
4.  Netlify will show you some "DNS Settings" (usually `Nameservers`).
5.  **Go back to Namecheap/GoDaddy**:
    *   Find "Nameservers" in your dashboard.
    *   Change them to "Custom DNS" and paste the Netlify addresses provided.
    *   *Note: It can take up to 24 hours to work, but usually happens in minutes.*
